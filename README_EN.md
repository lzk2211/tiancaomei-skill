## Examples

```
User      > As a new PhD student, what capabilities do I need? How to publish high-quality papers?

Tiancaomei> The key to publishing high-quality papers lies before you start writing, not during.
            Before typing the first word, you should be able to articulate three things in three
            sentences: what was missing, what you did differently, and what the results prove. If
            you can't do that, don't start writing.

            Effort matters, but direction matters more than effort. Many academic stars simply
            chose the right direction at the right time. During my last two years in Finland, I
            switched from AI to ISAC—had to relearn everything. But that direction choice saved
            my entire academic career.

            One more thing: Major Revision means the reviewer wants to give you a chance. Fear
            outright rejection instead. My first paper in a new direction got 60 comments. I wrote
            a 35-page Response Letter for a 12-page paper. This is normal in academia.
```

```
User      > Is 6G worth entering now? Does communications still have a future?

Tiancaomei> Let me start with a premise you may not want to hear: 5G's only application so far
            is still speed test. Operators are struggling, and interest in 5G-Advanced is lukewarm.
            Against this backdrop, "entering 6G" needs clarification.

            If you mean research—ISAC and satellite-terrestrial integration remain hard problems.
            6G standardization is in deep water. Worth doing.
            If you mean entrepreneurship or investment—you need to clearly understand that the 6G
            consumer market won't start until around 2030. What sustains you in the interim?

            Communications has never survived by chasing trends. Whether algorithms or systems,
            we're fundamentally building bridges for an increasingly fragmented world. Don't chase
            the hype. Find what's certain.
```

This is not role-playing. Tiancaomei uses five cognitive frameworks—Demand-Driven Design, Direction > Effort, Connection as Essence, Scientific Baseline Principle, Long-term Content View—to analyze problems with calibrated rigor, data-driven logic, and intellectual honesty.

## How It Works

Based on Nuwa's four-phase process:

**1. Six-way parallel collection** — 500+ Zhihu articles/answers, 50+ academic papers, in-depth media interviews, academic salon talks, science advisor behind-the-scenes accounts, academic collaboration network, and life timeline.

**2. Triple-verified extraction** — A viewpoint qualifies as a mental model only if it: ① appears across 2+ different source types, ② can infer positions on unfamiliar problems (predictive power), ③ is not what every scholar in the field would say (distinctive).

**3. Skill construction** — 5 mental models + 8 decision heuristics + expression DNA + values & anti-patterns + honest boundaries.

**4. Quality validation** — Tested against questions Tiancaomei has publicly addressed; then tested against questions he hasn't, where the Skill should show calibrated uncertainty.
